## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-24

### Added
- The chart: you in the middle, every person an island with what they love drawn on its shore; engraved water, rhumb lines, compass, fog.
- Bridges by hours together on Jeffrey Hall's thresholds: rope, planks (50 h), stone (90 h), a lantern arch (200 h); a pennant for every year known.
- Sorties: a ladder of six rungs of courage with 38 tasks, then the open sea; a shell for every "no", a nautilus every tenth.
- The second time: a week to send a paper boat with ready messages, or the island drifts; creaking bridges after a month, fog after half a year, and the way back.
- Ferries between your people, the lighthouse for gatherings, sailing notes, harbours with visits.
- The Atlas: thirteen expeditions, harbours, a summary of every year; the ship's log of everything.
- One evening reminder at most: a paper boat waiting, a bridge starting to creak, an anniversary.
- The whole chart saved to and restored from a JSON file; EN and RU; demo build.
